Banana Bread

Moist and flavorful banana bread that's perfect for breakfast or as an afternoon snack. The best way to use up overripe bananas!

Prep Time 10 min
Cook Time 60 min
Servings 1 loaf

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe bananas, mashed
  • 1/3 cup melted butter
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1 large egg, beaten
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • Pinch of salt
  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • Optional: 1/2 cup chopped walnuts or chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease a 9x5 inch loaf pan.
  2. In a large bowl, mash the ripe bananas with a fork until smooth.
  3. Mix in the melted butter until well combined.
  4. Stir in the sugar, beaten egg, and vanilla extract.
  5. Sprinkle the baking soda and salt over the mixture and mix well.
  6. Add the flour and stir until just incorporated. Don't overmix.
  7. If using, fold in walnuts or chocolate chips.
  8. Pour batter into the prepared loaf pan.
  9. Bake for 60-65 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
  10. Cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then turn out onto a wire rack to cool completely.

Notes

The riper the bananas, the better! Look for bananas with lots of brown spots. This bread freezes well for up to 3 months—wrap tightly in plastic wrap and foil.
